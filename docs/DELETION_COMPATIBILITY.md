The following reduction was developed for the eleven-element search. It avoids generating all abstract extensions of every represented parent. The completed computation gives **35 isomorphism classes**, specified in the [exact catalogue](../results/r4-n11/CATALOGUE.md).

**Deletion compatibility lemma.** Let E have n elements, let r be fixed, and let a nonempty family B of r-subsets of E be prescribed. Suppose n>2r and, for every e in E, the subfamily of B avoiding e is the basis family of a rank-r matroid on E-e. Then B is a matroid basis family.

Proof: take A,B in the proposed basis family and x in A-B. Since |A union B|<=2r<n, there is e outside A union B. Basis exchange in the deletion matroid on E-e supplies y in B-A with A-x+y a basis. This proves every ordered instance of the basis axiom. Nonemptiness was assumed. Consequently, for rank four on eleven elements, compatible representable deletion data already implies the matroid axioms. For sizes seven and eight, the implementation retains explicit basis axioms instead.

**A complete search from deletion parents.** An excluded minor of the target size is 3-connected. Deleting one element leaves a connected, simple, rank-four GF(5)-representable parent N on ten elements. Generate every such parent by starting with the four-element basis and extending represented matrices over GF(5), retaining every field-isomorphism class. Every simple rank-four representation has a basis, so this process covers it after change of coordinates and relabeling. Field-isomorphic parents have equivalent sets of represented extensions, so that deduplication is safe. Abstract isomorphism alone is not sufficient during represented generation.

For a fixed parent N, introduce a Boolean x_H for every rank-three hyperplane H of N. Its intended meaning is that a new element e does NOT lie in the closure of H. For an independent triple T of N, T+e is a basis exactly when x_cl(T) is true. A dependent triple never produces a four-element basis. Thus these variables determine the entire basis family of a prospective rank-preserving extension, and every such matroid extension has one of these assignments.

For each f of N, list every possible represented simple extension of N-f by e. Each is a finite Boolean pattern on the hyperplanes visible after removing f. Require the restriction of the assignment x to be one of these patterns. The deletion N is already represented. All eleven deletions are consequently rank-four represented matroids, so the lemma guarantees that the assignment defines a matroid M. Finally exclude every pattern that represents a GF(5) extension of N itself. The remaining models are exactly the simple deletion-minimal nonrepresentable extensions of N.

To test excluded-minor minimality, simplify each M/f and compare it with the complete catalogue of simple rank-three GF(5)-representable matroids on at most ten elements. A matroid is representable exactly when its simplification is representable, and these contractions have rank three. This checks all contractions, independently of any assumptions about the distribution of smaller excluded minors. Deduplicate the accepted matroids by exact matroid isomorphism.

**Complete labelled representation sets.** The represented generation catalogue is taken up to permutation of column labels as well as row operations and nonzero column scaling. A deletion table, however, requires all representations of a fixed labelled parent. For each abstract parent, align each unlabelled represented class using one isomorphism to that parent. Then close these representations under generators of the parent's full automorphism group. Normalize a fixed basis to the identity and normalize a deterministic spanning forest of the support graph to ones. These normal forms distinguish labelled projective classes. Every labelled representation is reached, because any two alignments of an unlabelled representative differ by an automorphism of the abstract parent. The automorphism group is computed from a colored element-versus-basis/nonbasis incidence graph.

**Represented extension tables.** There are 156 points in PG(3,5). For each labelled representation A of a parent, append every projective point not already used. The basis bit for T+e is the nonzero status of the 4-by-4 determinant on those columns. Cofactors of the three fixed columns of T give a linear form in the appended vector, allowing all 156 tests to be evaluated together in exact integer arithmetic modulo five. Duplicate patterns are merged. This yields all represented simple extensions and no others. The tables are encoded as shared reduced Boolean decision diagrams with exact Tseitin clauses.

The implementation files are `projective.py`, `table_sat.py`, `prepare_representations.py`, and `search_compatible_deletions.py`. Full classification requires complete parent catalogues, complete labelled representation orbits, exhaustion of every compatibility problem without UNKNOWN results, contraction checks, and global isomorphism deduplication. Small published classifications are used as end-to-end regression targets before any claim of completeness at size eleven.

**Independent finite-table join.** The same constraints can be solved without SAT. Store each relation as pairs `(coordinate_mask, allowed_patterns)`, with patterns expanded to the common hyperplane coordinates. To join two relations, group their rows by their assignment on the overlapping coordinates, then take every union of matching rows. The result is precisely their natural join. Iteration therefore enumerates every assignment satisfying all deletion constraints. Once the union of coordinates is complete, represented full patterns can be removed immediately; subsequent joins cannot make such a pattern nonrepresentable. If a prescribed intermediate-size or time bound is reached, the result is UNKNOWN and the worker falls back to SAT; an unresolved case prevents a completeness claim.

All hyperplane coordinates are covered by these deletion relations: a variable for a rank-three hyperplane H is absent after deleting f only if f is a coloop of the restriction N|H. Such a restriction has at most three coloops. Since N has ten elements, at least one deletion still sees the coordinate. In fact, any four different deletions cover all coordinates. This observation is a useful performance fact, although the program checks full coordinate coverage explicitly and imposes all deletion relations.

The independent join implementation is `table_join.py`. It has agreed with SAT on every connected nine-element search parent, and its entire ten-element labelled candidate set agrees with the SAT search: 5,991 candidates. Both methods reproduce exactly the published 130 ten-element rank-four excluded minors. Across sizes seven through ten, the calibration counts are 5, 92, 219, and 130, with exact isomorphism comparison against the published archive.

**Certificates and scope.** Completeness follows from represented-parent enumeration and the exhaustive table search, together with the lemmas above. Separately, every final matroid is to receive a determinant-CNF UNSAT certificate checked by DRAT-trim and explicit GF(5) matrices for all 22 immediate minors. The matrix checks use an independent modular Gaussian elimination implementation, as well as Sage. Checking immediate deletions and contractions suffices because field representability is closed under further minors. The certificate checks certify membership of each listed matroid; they do not by themselves certify completeness of the list.
